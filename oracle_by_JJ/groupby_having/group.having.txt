 select job,max(sal) from emp
 group by job;

 select job,avg(sal) from emp
 group by job

 select deptno,count(job) from emp
 group by deptno

By default, GROUP BY sorts the rows in ascending order based on the values in the group column.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
			HAVING CLAUSE

You use the HAVING clause to filter groups of rows

GROUP BY can be used without HAVING.

But HAVING must be used in  with GROUP BY.

You place the HAVING clause after your GROUP BY clause:

Having clause special use for give condition to group by like WHERE.


 select job,avg(sal) from emp
 group by job
 having avg(sal) between 2000 and 3000
